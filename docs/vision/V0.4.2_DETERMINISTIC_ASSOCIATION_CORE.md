# V0.4.2 — Deterministic Association Core

**Status:** implemented · **Engineering milestone:** V0.4.2 · **Product generation:** V1 ·
**Not production ready**

## Purpose

V0.4.1 built the vocabulary of a cross-camera comparison and the hard preconditions one must
clear, and stopped there: geometry could refuse a pair, never accept one, and `ASSOCIATED`
was unconstructible by design.

V0.4.2 builds the layer that decides. It accumulates short-lived evidence about candidate
pairs across venue instants and, when mutual, exclusive, unambiguous agreement has held long
enough and the wider group it would form does not contradict itself, says `ASSOCIATED`.
Anything less is `AMBIGUOUS` or `UNASSOCIATED`, recorded with the rule that refused it.

**It decides pairs; it still names nobody.** No `VenueSubjectId`, no identity registry, no
merge, no headcount and no duplicate suppression exists anywhere in this milestone. A
decision here concerns two tracklets at one instant. See
[What V0.4.2 does not do](#what-v042-does-not-do).

## The rule that shapes everything here

V0.4.1's rule still holds and is now load-bearing in the other direction: two people on one
square metre of floor are geometrically identical to one person seen twice, so **geometry
alone can never establish identity**. Admissibility is step 2 of six, and steps 3–6 exist
entirely to stop a coincidence in step 2 becoming an association.

A wrong merge corrupts every venue-level number above it and is harder to detect than a
missed one. Every threshold, every tie-break and every conflict rule in this milestone
therefore chooses the missed association: an unresolved group is refused whole, an
undecided pair stays `AMBIGUOUS`, and a broken run is earned again from zero.

## Architecture

```text
detection → camera-local tracking → calibration / spatial context
  → venue-time alignment / fan-in            (V0.4.0)
  → comparability gating + geometry          (V0.4.1)
  → deterministic association                ← V0.4.2 builds this
  → temporary venue-local identity           (later)
  → venue-level evidence → decision support  (later)
```

```text
services/vision/src/atreides_vision/association/
├── models.py      # + TrackletKey/CandidateKey, DecisionReason, DecisionConfidence,
│                  #   SupportSummary, PairDecision, AssociationRound
├── config.py      # + AssociationDecisionConfig — the only numbers that may authorise
├── history.py     # EvidenceSample, PairEvidence, PairEvidenceStore — bounded state
├── matching.py    # pure: per-camera preference, mutual agreement, group consistency
└── engine.py      # AssociationEngine — the facade the pipeline will orchestrate
```

The V0.4.1 boundaries are unchanged and still asserted by an AST walk over every module in
the package, recursively: association imports `behavior.coordinates`,
`behavior.models.SubjectRef`, `timing` and `errors` and nothing else — no tracker, no
detector, no calibration, no venue, no sibling signal layer, no model library, no pixel.

**`SubjectRef` is untouched.** It remains exactly `(camera_id, track_id)`. The new
`TrackletKey` is `(camera_id, stream_epoch, track_id)` — a sort and lookup key for evidence,
not an identity: it names a tracklet on one camera in one session, never a person.

**Geometry and timing are consumed, not reimplemented.** The engine calls
`gate_observation` and reads `GeometricEvidence`; there is no second separation, radius,
slack or simultaneity calculation in this milestone.

## The decision model

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `TrackletKey` / `CandidateKey` | `(camera_id, stream_epoch, track_id)` and a canonical pair of them — evidence keys and sort order |
| `EvidenceSample` | One measured instant for one pair: venue seconds, whether it agreed, trust, separation, rival margin |
| `PairEvidence` | The run of agreement in progress for one pair, as O(1) aggregates |
| `SupportSummary` | A frozen snapshot of that run: samples, total samples, span, start, last seen, weakest trust, worst separation, tightest rival margin |
| `DecisionReason` | Why a decision came out as it did — 8 values, one per rule that can refuse or accept |
| `DecisionConfidence` | `CORROBORATED` when every camera in the run was `VERIFIED`, else `PROVISIONAL` |
| `PairDecision` | One pair's outcome, its reasons, the assessment it was decided from, and its support |
| `AssociationRound` | One venue instant: the observations, every decision, in canonical order |

Over-claiming is unconstructible rather than merely discouraged. `PairDecision` refuses at
construction: an `ASSOCIATED` without support or confidence, an `ASSOCIATED` whose own
geometry excluded the pair, an `ASSOCIATED` between two tracklets on one camera, an
`ASSOCIATED` not carrying `SUSTAINED_MUTUAL_AGREEMENT`, a negative outcome borrowing that
reason, a decision stating no reason at all, a measured pair reported as `NOT_COMPARABLE`,
and a measured pair carrying no support.

## The exact positive-association rule

All six, at one instant, or the answer is not `ASSOCIATED`:

1. **Comparable** — every V0.4.1 hard gate passed.
2. **Admissible** — `separation_m ≤ position_tolerance_m + max_subject_speed_m_per_s ×
   latest_possible_gap_seconds`, from V0.4.1 unchanged.
3. **Mutual** — each is the other's *nearest admissible candidate on the other's camera*.
4. **Separated** — neither side's counterpart camera was declared ambiguous by gating, and
   the tighter of the two sides' rival margins is `None` (no rival at all) or
   `≥ acceptance_margin_m`.
5. **Sustained** — the unbroken run of instants satisfying 1–4 has
   `samples ≥ min_support_samples` **and** `span_seconds ≥ min_support_seconds`.
6. **Consistent** — the group this association would join contains no two tracklets on one
   camera and no two tracklets geometry positively excluded from each other.

Both halves of 5 are required because either alone is uninterpretable: a sample count means
different things at 2 FPS and 30 FPS, and a span alone can be two observations far apart.
Both bounds are inclusive (`≥`) — a gate excludes only what it can rule out.

### Outcome semantics

| Outcome | Means | Produced when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `NOT_COMPARABLE` | Nothing was measured | any hard gate refused the pair (`HARD_GATE_REJECTED`) |
| `UNASSOCIATED` | Measured, and geometry ruled it out | separation beyond the admissible radius (`GEOMETRY_EXCLUDED`) |
| `AMBIGUOUS` | Measured, not ruled out, not established | `SUPPORT_NOT_SUSTAINED`, `RIVAL_NOT_SEPARATED`, `NOT_MUTUAL_BEST`, `EXCLUSIVITY_CONFLICT`, `INCONSISTENT_EVIDENCE` |
| `ASSOCIATED` | All six conditions hold | `SUSTAINED_MUTUAL_AGREEMENT` |

`NOT_COMPARABLE` is still not a weaker `UNASSOCIATED`: a blind spot is not an absence of
people. `AMBIGUOUS` is the milestone's default answer, and a run that never resolves simply
stays there for as long as it is observed.

## Temporal confirmation

Evidence is a **run**, not a tally. Each measured instant either extends the run in progress
or ends it, and ending it resets the count, the start, the span and the rival margin to
zero. A run ends on:

- a contradicting instant — geometry excluded the pair, or agreement failed rules 3 or 4;
- a gap longer than `max_support_gap_seconds` since the pair was last observed;
- the pair ceasing to be comparable at all, which also **discards** the evidence entirely,
  since whatever was agreed happened under conditions that no longer hold.

A claim that two tracklets agreed for a second must not span an instant in which they did
not, so after any of these the association has to be earned again from zero.

What the run remembers is deliberately the pessimistic side of what it saw: the **weakest**
geometry trust, the **worst** separation and the **tightest** rival margin inside it. Those
are aggregates — the memory a pair costs does not grow with how long it is watched.

`SupportSummary.confidence` is `CORROBORATED` only if every contributing instant had
`VERIFIED` geometry on both cameras; an `UNVERIFIED` camera anywhere in the run makes the
association `PROVISIONAL`. Degraded integrity lowers what the association is worth instead
of being swallowed, and `UNTRUSTED` never reaches here at all — it is a hard gate.

## Exclusivity, ambiguity and conflict

**Exclusivity is structural, not a post-pass.** A preference is *per counterpart camera* and
names the single nearest admissible candidate on it, so at one instant one observation
cannot positively agree with two tracklets on the same camera. There is no assignment
solver, no Hungarian step and no global optimum: a maximum-weight matching would resolve
ties by picking the cheapest total, which is a guess presented as a result.

**Ambiguity is inherited and re-checked.** Gating already reports which counterpart cameras
offered candidates it could not separate; a pair touching one of those cameras is refused
`RIVAL_NOT_SEPARATED` whatever its own margin. Above that, the decision layer applies its
own, stricter `acceptance_margin_m` to the tighter of the two sides' runner-up gaps.

**A one-sided preference is not agreement.** If A prefers B but B prefers someone else, the
pair is `NOT_MUTUAL_BEST` — no partial credit, no "best available" fallback.

**A conflicting group is refused whole.** When accepted pairs form a group containing two
tracklets on one camera, every pair in that group becomes `AMBIGUOUS /
EXCLUSIVITY_CONFLICT`. Refusing the group rather than choosing a victim is both
deterministic and conservative: a group whose own evidence disagrees is not the place to
guess which part was right.

## Multi-camera consistency

Agreement is pairwise; its consequences are not. A agreeing with B and B with C would join A
and C without anybody having compared them. So before any association is emitted, the
connected groups formed by the accepted pairs are checked against the evidence that already
exists:

- two members on one camera → `EXCLUSIVITY_CONFLICT`;
- two members that *were* compared and geometry excluded from each other →
  `INCONSISTENT_EVIDENCE`.

Either refuses the entire group. This is complete-link protection: transitivity is not
evidence, and a chain of pairwise agreements cannot silently produce a merge nobody
measured.

**A pair that was never comparable is not a contradiction.** Cameras with no shared
calibrated region are a supported deployment, not a fault, and treating "never measured" as
"measured and disagreed" would make association impossible exactly where coverage is
sparsest. Nothing here knows or assumes how many cameras exist; three, four or forty
cameras run the same code.

## Expiry and bounded state

The engine's entire state is `PairEvidenceStore`, keyed by `CandidateKey`, with two limits,
both required:

- **TTL** — a pair unobserved for more than `evidence_ttl_seconds` of venue time is dropped.
  A config invariant refuses `evidence_ttl_seconds < max_support_gap_seconds`, which would
  discard evidence while the run it belongs to was still allowed to continue.
- **Ceiling** — beyond `max_tracked_pairs`, the least-recently-observed entries are evicted,
  ties breaking on the canonical key, so a full store behaves identically on two runs of the
  same input. Pinned by a test that churns 500 pairs and asserts exactly which survive.

Expiry runs on venue seconds, never a wall clock. Evidence is discarded outright when a pair
stops being comparable, and the whole store dies with the process: it is operational state,
not a memory of people, and nothing in it outlives the session or names anybody.

## Determinism

Observations are sorted by `(camera_id, stream_epoch, track_id, frame_index)`, pairs are
decided in sorted `CandidateKey` order, groups are built from sorted edges with a
deterministic union-find tie-break, preference tables are **looked up, never iterated**, and
every emitted sequence is sorted. No wall clock, no randomness and no `set`/`dict` iteration
order reaches an output.

The engine also refuses input that would make determinism meaningless: venue instants must
advance, and one tracklet may not appear twice at one instant. Tests pin byte-identical
records across two runs and across every permutation of camera enumeration order.

## Configuration

`AssociationDecisionConfig` — new in this milestone, deliberately separate from
`AssociationGatingConfig` so a venue can widen a rejection radius without loosening what it
takes to be believed. All are **development defaults; none was measured against real
multi-camera footage.**

| Parameter | Default | Unit | Errs toward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `min_support_samples` | 3 | agreeing observations | missed association (higher = stricter); refuses `< 2` |
| `min_support_seconds` | 0.4 | seconds of venue time | missed association |
| `max_support_gap_seconds` | 1.0 | seconds | missed association (shorter = runs break sooner) |
| `acceptance_margin_m` | 1.0 | metres | missed association (higher = demands a clearer winner) |
| `evidence_ttl_seconds` | 5.0 | seconds | bounded memory; must be `≥ max_support_gap_seconds` |
| `max_tracked_pairs` | 4096 | pairs | bounded memory |

Loosening any of the first four trades missed associations for false ones, which is the
wrong direction. No default here encodes a camera count, a frame rate, a resolution, a venue
size or a topology.

## Interfaces

No schema was added and **no frozen V0.3.x schema was changed**: behaviour stays `0.3.3`,
interaction `0.3.4`, crowd `0.3.5`, validation manifest `0.3.6`, venue session `0.4.0`.

`AssociationRound.to_record()` and `PairDecision.to_record()` exist for observability and
resolve every decision back to `(camera_id, track_id, stream_epoch, frame_index)` per side
with its evidence, reasons and support — but there is still **no `association/reporting.py`,
no JSONL stream and no `schema_version`**, for the same reason as V0.4.1: a versioned
contract is a promise, and the consumer of association output is the identity layer that
does not exist yet.

There is no CLI command and no pipeline wiring. Nothing in a running session calls this
package.

### Provenance

Fusion here is additive and nothing is lost. `PairDecision` holds the whole
`PairAssessment`, which holds both whole `TrackObservation`s, so every decision resolves
back to its contributing `(camera, track, stream_epoch, frame)` observations with the
separation, the radius it was judged against, the timing slack, the trust and the support
run that produced it. A refusal is as traceable as an acceptance.

## Validation

**Synthetic only.** Every behaviour in this milestone is established by unit tests over
hand-built observations with analytically known answers. That proves the rules; it says
nothing about occlusion, crowding, distance, glare or a real venue.

- Human-verified ground truth: **`NOT_ANNOTATED`.** No multi-camera association has been
  checked against a human annotation, because no genuinely simultaneous multi-camera footage
  exists in this repository.
- Proxy/system metrics: **none emitted.** No association rate, no merge count, no false-merge
  rate is produced by any pipeline, because no pipeline runs this code.
- Synthetic correctness: **87 new tests** (association total 304 across eight files).

| File | New | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `test_association_models.py` | 22 | Tracklet/candidate keys, support coherence, the over-claims that must be impossible to construct, round ordering |
| `test_association_history.py` | 24 | Run extension, contradiction, gaps, replay refusal, pessimistic aggregates, sustained boundaries, TTL, ceiling, 500-pair churn |
| `test_association_matching.py` | 18 | Per-camera preference, mutual/one-sided agreement, margin boundary, ambiguity veto, group consistency, canonical refusals |
| `test_association_engine.py` | 23 | Temporal confirmation, provenance, refusals, 3-camera consistency, exclusivity, expiry, single camera, determinism, input validation |

The failure directions are covered explicitly, not only the happy path: a true association
earned only after the run is sustained, a genuine non-match left `UNASSOCIATED`, a rival too
close left `AMBIGUOUS`, a one-sided preference refused, a transitive group refused whole in
both conflict flavours, a contradicting instant forcing the run to be earned again, evidence
expiring, a single camera returning `NOT_COMPARABLE` with no evidence at all, provenance
recoverable from the record after a decision, and byte-identical records across two runs and
across every camera ordering.

## What V0.4.2 does not do

- **No `VenueSubjectId` and no venue-local identity.** No type, no namespace, no registry,
  no mapping from `SubjectRef` to anything above it.
- **No identity fusion, no canonical identity, no merge state**, no subject store.
- **No duplicate-occupancy suppression** and no venue-level headcount or unique-visitor
  figure. Associated pairs are *not* collapsed anywhere.
- **No appearance embeddings, no ReID, no colour histogram, no crop, no biometric.**
- **No long-gap or non-overlap hand-off matching**, and no camera transition-time model.
  Association here is strictly between simultaneous observations.
- **No same-camera fragment stitching.** Crossing a stream epoch still breaks track identity.
- **No learned or trained association**, no GPU dependence, no weights, no network.
- **No venue-level crowd or interaction fusion.** Those layers remain camera-local.
- **No Risk / Decision Engine** and no decision-support output.
- **No pipeline wiring, no CLI command, no JSONL schema.**
- **No real-world multi-camera validation claim, and no production-readiness claim.**

## Limitations

- **No real-world validation whatsoever.** Synthetic correctness is not real-world
  robustness. Nothing here has met a real occlusion, a real crowd, a real calibration or two
  real cameras that overlap. Every threshold below is a development default.
- **`acceptance_margin_m`, `min_support_samples` and `min_support_seconds` are unmeasured.**
  They were chosen to be defensible and to err toward refusal. What they cost in missed
  associations on real CCTV is unknown, and cannot be known from this repository.
- **Association is instantaneous-only.** A pair must be simultaneously visible and
  admissible for a sustained run. A person walking between two non-overlapping cameras is
  never associated, by design — that is the hand-off problem, explicitly out of scope.
- **A run is broken by a single contradicting instant.** One frame of tracker jitter, one
  occluded foot placement or one moment where a rival passes close resets the count. This is
  the conservative direction, and it makes association in dense scenes correspondingly
  harder; no hysteresis, decay or tolerance for isolated disagreement exists.
- **Group refusal is all-or-nothing.** A four-tracklet group containing one contradiction
  loses every pair in it, including pairs whose own evidence was clean. Refusing whole is
  deterministic and safe; it is also blunt, and a venue with dense overlap may lose good
  associations to one bad one.
- **Consistency is checked only against pairs that were actually assessed.** Two members of
  a group that a hard gate never allowed to be compared cannot contradict each other, so a
  group spanning cameras with no shared calibrated region is checked less strictly than one
  where every pair was measured. That is the honest reading of "never measured", but it does
  mean the strength of the consistency check varies with coverage.
- **Confidence is two-valued.** `PROVISIONAL` / `CORROBORATED` reflects geometry trust only.
  It is not a probability, is not calibrated, and does not encode how much better the leader
  was than the rival or how long the run held — those are in the support summary, unfused.
- **Cost is O(N²) per instant, and each pair is assessed twice.**
  `gate_observation` is called once per observation against the whole pool, so every pair is
  measured from both sides and the engine keeps the first. Fine at the scales tested;
  unmeasured at forty cameras in a crowd, and unmeasured against any real workload, because
  nothing runs this code in a pipeline.
- **Evidence keys are pairs, so the store grows with the square of concurrent tracks.** The
  ceiling bounds it, but a venue that reaches the ceiling is evicting evidence that a run
  may have needed, and nothing reports that this happened beyond the drop count.
- **Trust is still supplied, not verified**, and alignment is still only as good as the
  operator's declaration — both inherited unchanged from V0.4.1 and V0.4.0. A confidently
  wrong `start_offset_seconds` produces confidently wrong venue times, and this layer will
  sustain a run on them without suspicion.
- **A permissive gate is still a permissive gate.** Under poor alignment the admissible
  radius grows without limit, so rules 3–5 carry the whole weight of the decision. They are
  the right rules for that job, but nothing refuses to decide on the grounds that the
  geometry underneath has stopped discriminating.

## Next milestone

V0.4.3 — temporary venue-local identity — consumes `AssociationRound` and is the first layer
permitted to create a `VenueSubjectId`. Its inputs are already conservative and already
carry their provenance and confidence; what it adds is the identity namespace, the
session-scoped registry, the rules for splitting an identity when its evidence stops

with **false merges as the most serious class**.
