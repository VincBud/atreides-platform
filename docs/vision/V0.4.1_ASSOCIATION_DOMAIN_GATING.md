# V0.4.1 — Association Domain & Comparability Gating

**Status:** implemented · **Engineering milestone:** V0.4.1 · **Product generation:** V1 ·
**Not production ready**

## Purpose

V0.4.0 built the time domain: N camera streams on one session-relative venue timeline, each
frame carrying its stream provenance and the uncertainty of its own clock. It coordinates
time and provenance and deliberately **does not compare people**.

V0.4.1 builds the layer immediately above it — the vocabulary in which a cross-camera
comparison is expressed, and the **hard preconditions** such a comparison must clear before
it may be attempted at all. When it is done, Atreides can state, for one camera-local
observation at one venue instant, which observations on other cameras it *may* be compared
with, which it may not and exactly why, which are excluded by the geometry, and which
survive as candidates for a later decision.

**It refuses; it never merges.** No venue-local identity, no fused subject, no merge state
and no headcount exists anywhere in this milestone. See
[What V0.4.1 does not do](#what-v041-does-not-do).

## The one rule that shapes everything here

Two people standing on the same square metre of floor at the same instant, seen by two
cameras, are **geometrically identical** to one person seen twice. There is no distance,
tolerance or clock that separates those two cases.

Agreement in space and time is therefore a *precondition* for believing two tracklets are
the same person, and never a *reason* to. So:

- Geometry can **veto** a merge. It can never **authorise** one.
- `AssociationOutcome.ASSOCIATED` is defined — the vocabulary is the contract, and a
  consumer written against three outcomes would break when the fourth arrives — but
  **nothing in V0.4.1 produces it**. `ObservationGating` raises rather than being
  constructed with it, so the guarantee is structural, not a convention a future edit could
  forget.
- Per `CLAUDE.md`, a wrong merge corrupts every venue-level number above it and is harder to
  detect than a missed one. Every default and every tie-break in this milestone chooses the
  missed association.

## Architecture

```text
detection → camera-local tracking → calibration / spatial context
  → venue-time alignment / fan-in            (V0.4.0)
  → comparability gating + geometry          ← V0.4.1 builds this
  → deterministic association                (later)
  → temporary venue-local identity           (later)
  → venue-level evidence → decision support  (later)
```

```text
services/vision/src/atreides_vision/association/
├── models.py      # AssociationOutcome, GateReason/GateRejection, GeometryTrust,
│                  # VenueRegion, CameraSpatialContext, TrackObservation,
│                  # CandidatePair, GeometricEvidence, PairAssessment, ObservationGating
├── config.py      # AssociationGatingConfig — every number that can reject a candidate
├── geometry.py    # pure maths: venue-time gaps, timing slack, separation, radii
├── gates.py       # the hard gates, in one fixed evaluation order
└── candidates.py  # deterministic enumeration, per-pair assessment, per-observation gating
```

Four boundaries are load-bearing.

**Cross-camera logic lives here and nowhere else.** No tracker, tracker adapter, detector or
camera-local analyzer knows another camera exists. A test asserts that no module under
`tracking/`, `detection/`, `behavior/`, `interaction/`, `crowd/`, `zones/` or `venue/`
imports this package.

**Association imports values, not machinery.** The package reaches only
`behavior.coordinates` (the shared coordinate vocabulary), `behavior.models.SubjectRef`,
`timing` and `errors`. It imports **no** calibration, venue, video, detection, tracking or
sibling signal layer, and no model library. That is what keeps OpenCV — and every pixel —
out of this layer transitively: `calibration.integrity` reaches `cv2` through its
registration backend, and `venue` reaches it through `video.source`. Asserted by an AST walk
over every module in the package, recursively, including any future subpackage.

**`SubjectRef` is untouched.** It remains exactly `(camera_id, track_id)`. Nothing was added
to it, and no venue-wide identifier exists to add.

**Nothing above the projector sees a pixel.** A `TrackObservation` carries a *projected*
position and has no image, no bounding box and no appearance field of any kind. Asserted on
the dataclass's field list, not left to review.

## Domain model

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `TrackObservation` | One camera-local track at one venue instant: `SubjectRef`, `StreamRef`, frame index, venue seconds, `TimeUncertainty`, projected `MotionPoint`, camera spatial context |
| `CameraSpatialContext` | What one camera's geometry is currently worth: space, venue frame, surface, `GeometryTrust`, optional calibrated region |
| `GeometryTrust` | `VERIFIED` · `UNVERIFIED` · `UNTRUSTED` — a narrowing of `IntegrityState`, passed in as a value |
| `VenueRegion` | The venue-metre footprint a camera's calibration covers, as an axis-aligned box |
| `CandidatePair` | Two observations in a canonical order, so `(A, B)` and `(B, A)` are one candidate |
| `GeometricEvidence` | Separation, the radius it was judged against and both terms of that radius, the nominal venue-time gap, the timing slack, and the pair's trust |
| `GateRejection` | One hard gate's refusal: a `GateReason` for a machine and a sentence for an operator |
| `PairAssessment` | One candidate: its rejections, or its evidence — never both, and never neither |
| `ObservationGating` | One observation against a pool: every pair considered, the survivors ranked, the cameras geometry could not resolve, and the outcome |

Consistency is enforced at construction rather than trusted: an observation whose subject,
stream and context name different cameras is refused; a position in a space its context does
not declare is refused; a rejected pair carrying a measurement is refused (numbers computed
across a closed gate get read later as though they meant something); a comparable pair
carrying *no* measurement is also refused (an unreported blind spot).

### Identity provenance

A candidate never loses where it came from. `CandidatePair` holds whole observations, and
`ObservationGating.to_record()` resolves every considered pair — survivor or refusal — back
to `(camera_id, track_id, stream_epoch, frame_index)` with the evidence or the rejection
reason attached. Crossing a stream epoch boundary still breaks track identity: two tracklets
on one camera either side of a reconnect are refused by the same-camera gate, like any other
same-camera pair.

## The hard gates

Evaluated in one fixed order, `HARD_GATE_ORDER`, and **every applicable gate is evaluated**
— not merely the first that fails. An operator whose two cameras never associate needs the
whole list: fixing the venue frame is wasted work if the surfaces also differ.

| # | `GateReason` | Refuses when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `SAME_CAMERA` | Both observations are on one camera |
| 2 | `NOT_GROUND_PLANE_METRES` | Either position is not in calibrated ground-plane metres |
| 3 | `DIFFERENT_VENUE_FRAME` | The two `venue_id`s differ |
| 4 | `DIFFERENT_SURFACE` | The two `surface_id`s differ |
| 5 | `CAMERA_GEOMETRY_UNTRUSTED` | Either camera's trust is `UNTRUSTED` (it moved) |
| 6 | `VENUE_TIME_NOT_ESTABLISHED` | Either stream's alignment is `NOT_ESTABLISHED` |
| 7 | `NOT_SIMULTANEOUS` | `nominal_gap − slack > max_pairing_gap_seconds` |
| 8 | `NO_SHARED_CALIBRATED_REGION` | Both regions declared, and their gap exceeds the admissible radius |

Gates that cannot be evaluated once an earlier one failed are **skipped, never guessed**: an
uncalibrated pair is not also accused of a venue mismatch (it has no venue frame), and a
pair with no common clock is judged on neither time nor region (there is no honest window
and no honest radius). A rejection list is therefore always a set of facts.

Any rejection ⇒ `NOT_COMPARABLE`. That is **not** a weaker `UNASSOCIATED`: nothing was
measured, so nothing was found, and a consumer that conflates them will report a blind spot
as an absence of people.

### Why 1 and 8 are gates rather than results

`SAME_CAMERA` — association is cross-camera by definition. Joining two tracklets on one
camera is *fragment stitching*: a different capability with different failure modes,
explicitly out of scope.

`NO_SHARED_CALIBRATED_REGION` — a fact about the two **cameras**, not about the people: if
their calibrated regions are further apart than any person could bridge in the time that may
separate the observations, no simultaneous association between them is possible at all.
**Non-overlapping cameras are an ordinary deployment, not an error**, and the gate only runs
when both operators declared a region — an undeclared region disables it rather than
assuming one.

## Geometry

```text
nominal_gap      = |venue_seconds_a − venue_seconds_b|
slack            = half_width_a + half_width_b           (None if either is NOT_ESTABLISHED)
earliest_gap     = max(0, nominal_gap − slack)           → the simultaneity gate
latest_gap       = nominal_gap + slack                   → the spatial radius
motion_allowance = max_subject_speed_m_per_s × latest_gap
admissible_radius= position_tolerance_m + motion_allowance
admissible       ⇔ separation_m ≤ admissible_radius
```

Separation bottoms out in `MotionPoint.distance_to`, the same primitive the interaction and
crowd layers use. There is no second distance implementation in Atreides.

**The boundary is permissive.** A pair sitting exactly on the radius is *not* excluded: a
gate excludes only what it can rule out.

## Outcome semantics

Assigned per observation by `gate_observation`, and per pair by
`PairAssessment.geometric_outcome`.

| Outcome | Meaning | Produced by V0.4.1? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `NOT_COMPARABLE` | No pair cleared the hard gates — including the empty pool | yes |
| `UNASSOCIATED` | Something was comparable and geometry excluded all of it | yes |
| `AMBIGUOUS` | Some counterpart camera offers candidates geometry cannot separate | yes |
| `None` | Geometry has no verdict; candidates carried forward, nearest first | yes |
| `ASSOCIATED` | The evidence supports treating these as one person | **never** |

`None` is not a fifth outcome. It is the honest absence of one: the pair survived, and
whether it is one person is a question for evidence this layer does not have.

### Ambiguity is assessed per counterpart camera

Two candidates on **different** cameras are not rivals — a person standing in three cameras'
views produces three mutually consistent observations, and calling that ambiguous would
penalise good coverage. Two candidates on the **same** counterpart camera are rivals: at one
instant, one person is one track on one camera.

So ambiguity is decided within each counterpart camera, comparing its two nearest
candidates: if they differ by less than `ambiguity_margin_m`, that camera is reported in
`ambiguous_cameras` and the outcome is `AMBIGUOUS`. **Both candidates are retained** —
picking the nearer of two indistinguishable candidates is a coin flip presented as a fact.
Nothing here knows or assumes how many cameras exist.

## Timing semantics

V0.4.0 deliberately gave `TimeUncertainty` no threshold, no comparison operator and no
`is_aligned_enough()`, and named `worst_of` as one step from "aligned enough to merge?".
V0.4.1 is the layer that takes that step, and takes it **here** rather than in the shared
time vocabulary: `timing.py` and `venue/` are unchanged, and the only place a half-width
meets a threshold is the association layer that owns the question.

Two rules keep it honest.

**`NOT_ESTABLISHED` refuses rather than defaults.** A stream nobody aligned has
`half_width_seconds is None`, which is not `0.0` and does no arithmetic. Any pair involving
one is `NOT_COMPARABLE` on gate 6. A shared `venue_micros` key is a *scheduling* fact;
without an established alignment it is not evidence of a shared moment, exactly as V0.4.0
states.

**Uncertainty weakens a gate; it never strengthens one.** The simultaneity gate gives the
pair the full slack in its favour and refuses only if even the best case is outside the
window. The spatial radius takes the worst case and widens. Two badly aligned cameras
therefore admit almost everything — and the admissible radius that admitted them is on the
record, so a pair that survived only because the clocks are ±30 s apart never looks like one
that survived on a tight metre. A gate that grew stricter as alignment degraded would be
deriving a rejection from precision nobody established: the same laundering V0.4.0 exists to
prevent.

An `ASSUMED` frame rate needs no gate of its own. V0.4.0 already forces such a stream's
`TimeUncertainty` to `NOT_ESTABLISHED`, so gate 6 catches it.

## Calibration and integrity semantics

Cross-camera geometry requires `GROUND_PLANE_METRES` on both sides, the same `venue_id`, and
the same `surface_id`. Normalized image coordinates are camera-local by construction — a
frame fraction on one camera has no relationship to one on another — and no physical
distance is ever derived from an uncalibrated scene. There is no normalized-space variant of
the config and no `intended_space` knob: there is nothing to fall back to.

Integrity enters as `GeometryTrust`, a deliberate narrowing of `IntegrityState` performed by
whoever owns the integrity guard and passed in as a value:

```text
CONSISTENT            → VERIFIED
UNCHECKED             → UNVERIFIED
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE → UNVERIFIED
DRIFT_DETECTED        → UNTRUSTED
```

`UNTRUSTED` is a hard gate — a camera that moved stops contributing rather than contributing
quietly, matching `IntegrityAssessment.allows_metric_output`. `UNCHECKED` and
`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` collapse into one level because neither is evidence that the camera
moved and neither is evidence that it did not; what they share — nobody confirmed the
viewpoint — is exactly what a consumer of a geometric comparison needs to know. An
unverified camera still compares, and the pair's evidence carries the **weaker** of the two
cameras' trust, so degraded integrity lowers what the comparison is worth rather than being
swallowed. Since no merge happens in this milestone at all, that is the whole consequence
available today; a decision layer must read `GeometricEvidence.trust`.

## Determinism

Output order is fixed by `(camera_id, stream_epoch, track_id, frame_index)` — `camera_id`
comparing by Python's default string ordering, never locale and never insertion order,
matching the venue scheduler's rule. Candidates rank by `(separation_m, order_key)`, so ties
in distance break on identity rather than on arrival order, and `ambiguous_cameras` is
sorted.

Cameras supplied in any order produce identical candidates, identical assessments, identical
outcomes and byte-identical records. This is pinned by tests over **every permutation** of a
four-camera pool, not merely over one reversal. An association that depends on camera
enumeration order is one nobody can reproduce or falsify.

## Bounded state

There is none. Gating is three pure functions over values; nothing is retained between
calls, no history is kept, no cache exists. The package holds exactly **one** module-level
container — the trust rank map — which a test pins by name and re-measures after churning
500 distinct track ids, because a cache appearing here is how unbounded state starts.

## Scalability

Nothing hardcodes a camera count, a layout, a naming convention, a coverage area, a venue
size, an overlap pattern, a mounting geometry, a frame rate or a resolution. Cameras are
whatever the observations name. Topology is never assumed: the only spatial relationship
this layer knows is the one an operator's own calibration correspondences describe, and it
is optional.

**Single camera is the same code path.** One camera produces no candidate pairs, so every
observation gates to `NOT_COMPARABLE`: there is no cross-camera evidence to have, and the
layer says so instead of erroring, branching or inventing one. There is no `if
multi_camera:` anywhere.

## Interfaces

No schema was added and **no frozen V0.3.x schema was changed**: behaviour stays `0.3.3`,
interaction `0.3.4`, crowd `0.3.5`, validation manifest `0.3.6`, venue session `0.4.0`.

`to_record()` exists on the domain types so a rejection and its reason can be serialised for
observability, but there is deliberately **no `association/reporting.py`, no JSONL stream and
no `schema_version`**. A versioned contract is a promise to downstream consumers; there is no
association consumer yet, and the shape of one depends on the decision layer that does not
exist. Adding it now would freeze a contract before anyone has designed it.

There is no CLI command. Nothing in the pipeline calls this package: V0.4.0 deliberately left
per-camera signal wiring in a venue session unbuilt, and wiring association into a running
session needs that first.

## Validation

**Synthetic only.** Every figure and behaviour in this milestone is established by unit tests
over hand-built observations with analytically known answers. That proves the formulas and
the refusals; it says nothing about occlusion, crowding, distance, glare or a real venue.

- Human-verified ground truth: **`NOT_ANNOTATED`.** No multi-camera association has been
  checked against a human annotation, because no genuinely simultaneous multi-camera footage
  exists in this repository — the V0.3.6 clips are independent captures.
- Proxy/system metrics: **none emitted.** No association rate, no merge count, no candidate
  statistics are produced by any pipeline, because no pipeline runs this code.
- Synthetic correctness: 208 tests across five files.

| File | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| `test_association_models.py` | Domain invariants; the over-claims that must be impossible to construct |
| `test_association_geometry.py` | Gaps, slack, separation, radii — every expectation computed by hand |
| `test_association_gates.py` | Each hard gate in isolation, gate order, skipped-not-guessed |
| `test_association_candidates.py` | Enumeration, true pair survives, non-match refused, ambiguity boundaries, single camera, no overlap, provenance, determinism, no retained state |
| `test_association_boundaries.py` | Import boundaries, no lower layer imports association, no identity-shaped name, guards proven to fail when they should |

The failure directions are covered explicitly: a true pair kept, a genuine non-match
excluded, an ambiguous pair left unmerged with both candidates retained, a boundary case
exactly on the radius and one a hair beyond, a boundary case exactly on the ambiguity margin
and one a hair inside, provenance recoverable from the record after gating, and identical
results across two runs and across all 24 orderings of a four-camera pool.

## What V0.4.1 does not do

- **No association decision.** Nothing decides that two tracklets are one person. `ASSOCIATED`
  is unreachable by construction.
- **No `VenueSubjectId` and no temporary venue-local identity.** No type, no namespace, no
  mapping from `SubjectRef` to anything above it.
- **No identity fusion or merge state**, no subject store, no session-scoped identity table.
- **No duplicate-occupancy suppression** and no venue-level headcount or unique-visitor
  figure.
- **No appearance embeddings, no ReID, no colour histogram, no crop, no biometric.** An
  observation carries a projected floor position and nothing that could describe a person.
- **No long-gap or non-overlap hand-off matching**, and no camera transition-time model.
- **No same-camera fragment stitching.** Crossing a stream epoch still breaks track identity.
- **No learned or trained association**, no GPU dependence, no weights, no network.
- **No venue-level crowd or interaction fusion.** Those layers remain strictly camera-local.
- **No Risk / Decision Engine** and no decision-support output.
- **No pipeline wiring, no CLI command, no JSONL schema.**
- **No production-readiness claim.**

## Limitations

- **No real-world validation whatsoever.** Synthetic correctness is not real-world
  robustness. Nothing here has met a real occlusion, a real crowd, a real calibration or two
  real cameras that overlap.
- **`position_tolerance_m` is the dominant free parameter and is unmeasured.** 1.5 m is a
  development default chosen to be defensible, not a measured figure for how far two
  calibrated cameras place one person. Too small silently rejects true pairs; too large
  surfaces as ambiguity, which is the safer direction and why the default errs high.
- **`max_subject_speed_m_per_s` is a plausibility bound, not a model of movement.** It is set
  near the human sprint limit on purpose. It does not know about doors, walls, barriers or
  crowds, so the radius it produces is generous by construction.
- **The calibrated region is a lower bound on coverage, used as if it were the coverage.** It
  is built from correspondences an operator supplied, and a camera calibrated against one
  doorway may view half a hall. The gate can therefore refuse a pair of cameras that really
  do share a view outside their calibrated regions — a missed association, the acceptable
  direction, but a real one. The region is an axis-aligned box rather than a convex hull for
  the same reason: the box is a superset, so the simplification can only under-reject.
- **Trust is supplied, not verified.** `GeometryTrust` is whatever the caller passes.
  Nothing here checks that a camera claiming `VERIFIED` was ever checked, and no composition
  root performs the `IntegrityState → GeometryTrust` mapping yet, because nothing wires this
  layer into a pipeline.
- **Alignment is only ever as good as the operator's declaration**, inherited unchanged from
  V0.4.0. A confidently declared but wrong `start_offset_seconds` produces confidently wrong
  venue times, and this layer will gate on them without suspicion. Atreides cannot measure
  the offset between two streams and does not try — that would be association by another
  name.
- **A permissive gate is a deliberate trade.** Under poor alignment the admissible radius
  grows without limit and the geometry discriminates nothing. The radius is recorded so this
  is visible, but nothing refuses to gate on the grounds that gating has become useless, and
  no threshold on the half-width itself exists to make it do so.
- **Ambiguity is decided on the two nearest candidates per camera.** Three mutually
  indistinguishable candidates report the same `AMBIGUOUS` as two, and the count of rivals is
  not summarised anywhere except in the candidate list itself.
- **Cost is O(N²) in the observations compared.** `candidate_pairs` enumerates every
  unordered pair, and `gate_observation` assesses one observation against the whole pool.
  There is no spatial index and no early exit. Fine at the scales tested; unmeasured at
  forty cameras in a crowd, and unmeasured against any real workload, because nothing runs
  this code in a pipeline yet.
- **`AssociationOutcome` is defined for a decision this milestone cannot make.** A reader who
  sees `ASSOCIATED` in the enum and assumes something produces it will be wrong. That risk is
  accepted for the contract's stability, and countered by making the value unconstructable
  in a gating result.

## Next milestone

V0.4.2 — deterministic association — sits directly on this seam: it consumes
`ObservationGating`, adds evidence beyond geometry, and is the first layer permitted to
produce `ASSOCIATED`. It is validated on its own terms, with **false merges as the most
serious class**, alongside missed associations, ambiguous cases, duplicate-count suppression
and provenance integrity.
